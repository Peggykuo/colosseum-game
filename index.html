Laws of the Colosseum

  1. Opposite Starts: Two gladiators begin on opposite ends of the arena (default 5 × 10, adjustable up to 50 × 50).
  2. Step or Hold: Each turn, click the board to move Up, Down, Left, Right, or Stay. You cannot step onto the other player's square or cross walls.
  3. Mandatory Wall: After choosing your square, click one of its open borders to erect a permanent wall. A wall must be placed every turn.
  4. Confirm — No Undo: Review your previewed move & wall on the board, then click Confirm & Lock Move. Confirmed moves are irreversible.
  5. Victory by Division: The instant walls completely separate the two gladiators, the game ends and the side with strictly more reachable squares wins.
